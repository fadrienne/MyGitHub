# Beyond the Reef Line: OCL Pitch Video, Final Script

Applicant: Francesca Adrienne | Grant theme: Educate (secondary: Inspire) | Request: £5,000
Runtime: 2:00 including a 3-second Kreol clip and a 3-second silent title card. Voiceover: 276 words at 145 words per minute. Shot timings are computed from the words.

## What the video says

1. The problem: Seychelles has 115 islands and 1.35 million square kilometres of ocean, and much of its ocean science stays locked in technical papers.
2. The book: a print and open-access digital book for students aged 12 to 18 (secondary 1 to post-secondary), bringing existing content from separate silos into one narrative, shore to deep sea.
3. Why you: grew up at Beau Vallon, 12 years in GIS, led Seychelles' maritime boundary delimitation, founded a hydrospatial NGO.
4. The grant: £5,000 funds 100 printed copies, the digital edition, QR audio and video, and workshops across Mahé, Praslin and La Digue. The 50-participant workshops are pilots. The print copies reach every school and library on the three islands. The digital edition does the wide reach: 500 downloads and 200 QR scans.
5. The test: a before-and-after survey six months apart. Success is at least half the students more interested in an ocean science career.
6. The ask: £5,000 to launch it. The digital edition stays open access.

In Seychelles, 115 islands sit across more than 1.35 million square kilometres of ocean. Growing up by the shore, we know hauling the net on the beach, launching a pirogue at dawn, and harvesting tektek. Beyond the reef line, in the Amirante Trench where an ROV dived to 4,600 metres, much of our ocean science stays locked in technical papers.

Beyond the Reef Line is a print and open-access digital book for students aged 12 to 18. The content already exists, held in silos by scientists, conservation groups and expedition teams. This book brings it into one narrative, depth by depth, with colouring plates, QR audio and video, Kreol and English voices, and a Drawing Canvas where students, fishers, and families add their own horizon.

[0:55 to 1:04, Scene 3: Why me]

The print copies reach every school and library on the three islands, and the digital edition does the wide reach, with targets of 500 downloads and 200 QR scans.

## Fix before you submit

1. **The success test is weak.** Workshop students already like the subject, and there is no comparison group. Define "more interested" (for example, up at least one point on a five-point scale set at the first workshop) and say whether half means half of the 50 or half of those who reply. By six months some will not reply.
2. **Does the grant period cover the six-month follow-up?** If the project must report sooner, say who funds the follow-up.
3. **Who hosts the digital edition after the grant, and who pays?** The video now promises it stays open access. Name the host and the annual cost in the application.
4. **The premise has no proof.** "Locked in technical papers" is the whole argument. One number would help: count the Seychelles ocean science titles on the library shelves you filmed, or ask ten teachers.
5. **Libraries.** Decided: no further library images and no larger originals for the school images. Schools from all three islands are in shot 4.3 (Mahé, Grand Anse Primary on Praslin, La Digue). The libraries shown are all on one island, so the spoken claim "every school and library on the three islands" rests on the narration alone. Image A shows about ten pupils in the full frame, so the video uses only its upper floor, and the full frame needs consent.
6. **Photos.** Confirm who took Beach 2, Beach 3, the Anse Royale sunrise, the school images A and B and the library photos, and that you may use them. The fishers and pupils in the full frames are identifiable, which is why only people-free crops are used.
7. **Words attributed to real people.** The QR audio scripts in the manuscript name nine real people. Do not show any of that text unless each person has approved their exact words. The comic portraits on p29 and p30 need consent too.
8. **Partner branding.** Do not show book pages with partner names or logos: p2, p6, p19, p20, p26, p32, and the banner on p35 (cropped in 2.5).
9. **Errors inside the book.** The trench is deeper than 5,200 m and the ROV reached 4,600 m (the script now says so). p30 says "four miles" and 4,600 m is about 2.9 miles. Pressure is 450 times on p29 and 460 times on p33. Saya de Malha is 7 to 50 m on p21 and 10 m on p26. The word search on p7 is not solvable as printed.
10. **Kreol.** Have a fluent speaker proofread the glossary and the recorded line, and confirm the English subtitle matches what is said.
11. **The QR code.** Replace the placeholder text with the real address once the microsite exists, and test a scan.
12. **Mockup binding.** The mockup shows a thick full-colour hardcover. Decide the binding and the per-copy cost, or the budget will not match what a reviewer sees.
13. **Real footage.** The phone clip, the mockup and some visuals are AI-generated placeholders. Replace the phone clip with your own phone scanning a printed page, and add one consented shot of people (for example, the tektek harvest) if you can.
14. **Funder criteria.** I have not seen the scoring criteria. Check the video uses their words.
15. **Credentials line.** Confirm "led Seychelles' maritime boundary delimitation" is how you want to put it.
